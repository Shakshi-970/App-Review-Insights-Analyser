## Recommended Actions

1. **Revert index display** — Restore the expanded Nifty/Sensex card layout or add a settings toggle. High urgency: multiple users threatening to switch apps.

2. **Order execution reliability** — Investigate and reduce latency during low-network conditions; add a retry/queue mechanism to prevent missed trades.

3. **Brokerage transparency** — Surface a clear brokerage breakdown before order confirmation to reduce post-trade surprise and negative reviews.

4. **Glitch investigation** — Conduct a targeted bug-bash on the top reported crashes/freezes to protect the "easy to use" reputation.

5. **Leverage positive NPS** — Users who call Groww "the best investment app" are candidates for in-app rating prompts and referral campaigns.
